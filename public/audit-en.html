Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we receive

Is the audit really free? What's the catch?+
No catch. For us, an audit is the most effective way to show you what we can do - and for you the least risky way to test us. Some clients keep the report after the audit and we never hear from them again. That's fine - you left with concrete data for free and we don't stress you.
What if I'm not doing any advertising at all yet?+
For you, the audit is even more valuable. We'll prepare a potential analysis - how many people search for your services on Google monthly, what the competition is like, how much it would cost to start and what volume of inquiries you can realistically expect. Without this analysis, you'd be investing blindly.
Do I have to give access to the advertising account?+
Ideally yes - readonly access to Google Ads or Meta Ads account allows for deeper analysis. But we can work without it too - we analyze the website, competition, search potential and measurement from the outside. In the email after filling out the form, we'll send you the exact procedure.
How long does the audit take?+
You'll get the results within 48 hours of filling out the form. We do it manually, not automatically - that's why it takes a day and a half, not 5 minutes. No automated export from a tool.
We're a small company with a budget of €300/month. Does it make sense?+
Yes. We specialize precisely in small and medium-sized companies. With a small budget, every euro is more important - one bad campaign can "eat up" the entire monthly budget. That's exactly why audits have the greatest value for small companies.
We already have another agency. Does a second opinion make sense?+
A second opinion always makes sense - if you're satisfied with your current agency, the audit will confirm that (which is value in itself). When you have doubts, the audit will either dispel them or confirm them with concrete numbers. We don't ask if you're changing suppliers - we just show you how the campaigns are performing.
What are the next steps if we want to collaborate?+
After receiving the report, you can take your time. When you decide, write us back via email - we'll prepare a quote based on the scope (campaign management from €149/month, no commitment). No automatic sign-ups, no "discovery call" in the calendar.
